Earth

Our home planet

🌎 About Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known planet where life exists. It has liquid water, an atmosphere rich in nitrogen and oxygen, and a wide variety of ecosystems.

🔭 Earth Facts

☀️ Distance from Sun

About 150 million km

🌙 Moon

Earth has 1 natural moon.

🌡️ Average Temperature

About 15°C

⏱️ Length of a Day

About 24 hours

🕰️ Earth Before Humans

🌋

About 4.5 billion years ago

Earth formed from material surrounding the young Sun.

🌊

Early Earth

Earth cooled over time and oceans eventually formed.

🦠

Early Life

Simple forms of life appeared billions of years ago.

🦖

Age of Dinosaurs

Dinosaurs lived on Earth for millions of years before modern humans existed.

🧑

Humans

Modern humans appeared very recently compared with Earth's enormous history.

✨ Interesting Facts

  • About 71% of Earth's surface is covered by water.
  • Earth has one natural satellite, the Moon.
  • Earth is currently the only world known to support life.
  • Earth's atmosphere helps protect life from harmful radiation.
